Anime Expeditions beginner guide

A practical first-session route for Anime Expeditions, with progression priorities, common mistakes, and a clear stopping point.

Short answer

Start with the core loop, protect a small resource reserve, and improve one repeated task at a time. The six-step route below is designed for a first useful session, not a speedrun.

Your first useful session

1

Complete the first reliable expedition with the units already available before rerolling heavily.

2

Build a team with damage, area coverage, and support instead of filling every slot with the rarest-looking unit.

3

Level the units that remain useful across several modes.

4

Save Trait Crystals until a long-term team member has been identified.

5

Use friends to cover a missing role during difficult waves or bosses.

6

Redeem milestone and compensation codes, then record what each resource is for before spending it.

Four mistakes that slow beginners down

  • Rerolling traits on a unit that will soon leave the team.
  • Building only single-target damage and losing to dense waves.
  • Spending every summon resource before checking the current progression goal.
  • Using a strong unit in the wrong placement or upgrade order.

What to upgrade first

A best team is a set of roles, not six copies of the highest rarity. The right unit solves a wave, boss, range, or economy problem that the rest of the lineup cannot cover.

Primary area damage

Use broad coverage to prevent normal waves from leaking while resources are saved.

Boss damage

Keep at least one unit whose upgrades stay efficient against a single durable target.

Support or control

Slows, buffs, or other support effects can improve every damage slot at once.

Set a stopping point

Finish one complete objective, secure the resources earned, and stop before starting another long loop. This avoids wasting timed boosts and makes it easier to remember the next clear goal when you return.

Where to go next

The efficient route repeats the highest stage that is reliable, then spends resources on the next specific blockage. Random rerolls without a target make progress slower.
