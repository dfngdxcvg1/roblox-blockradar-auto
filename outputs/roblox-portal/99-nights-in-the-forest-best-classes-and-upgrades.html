Best 99 Nights classes and upgrades: what to buy first

Best 99 Nights classes and upgrades: what to buy first, with decision criteria, practical progression order, and mistakes that waste resources.

Short answer

The strongest early upgrade is the one that prevents a run from collapsing. Camp stability and day multipliers usually matter before a specialized class.

Best choices by purpose

Beds

Build these early because the day multiplier reduces how many full cycles the team must survive.

Fire resource reserve

A stocked camp protects every role; spending the final resource on exploration is rarely worth it.

Missing-child rescue progress

Rescues improve the multiplier and give daylight searches a concrete purpose.

A class that fills the missing role

Choose for gathering, support, or survival needs instead of buying solely by rarity.

Team communication

A return time and assigned jobs are effectively a free upgrade to every resource run.

Use this decision order

1

Name the exact problem: income, survival, range, capacity, wave coverage, or another bottleneck.

2

Reject any option that does not solve that problem at the current progression level.

3

Compare the full cost with the resources that must remain in reserve.

4

Choose the smallest upgrade that creates a noticeable improvement.

5

Test the result before committing the next scarce resource.

Avoid the rarity trap

Rare does not always mean useful now. An expensive item can be weaker for the current task, too costly to support, or difficult to trade. Keep the decision tied to the next repeatable objective.

When values or balance change

Recheck this page's date after a major update. Developers can change stats, prices, rewards, or progression requirements without preserving an old community ranking.
