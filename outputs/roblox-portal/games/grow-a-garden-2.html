Before you play

  1. Open the official Roblox page and confirm the experience name.
  2. Read the spending and scam notes before trading or buying passes.
  3. For younger players, preview chat behavior and scare level first.

Beginner route

  1. Buy a small mix of seeds instead of spending everything on one restock.
  2. Keep part of each harvest as a cash reserve.
  3. Learn the day and night loop before joining a guild race.
  4. Redeem watering-can codes only through the in-game code control.

Parent notes

  1. The official maturity label is Minimal.
  2. Stealing is a game mechanic and can create social friction.
  3. Offline growth and restock timers can encourage frequent checking.
