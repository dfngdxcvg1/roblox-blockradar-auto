Use values as a checkpoint, not a promise

A list can tell you whether two items are in roughly the same range. It cannot guarantee demand in your server, predict an update, or make an unsafe trade safe. Verify every item before confirming and never use a middleman supplied by a stranger.

Why demand changes the trade

Two items can have similar list values while taking very different amounts of time to trade. The demand signal helps you notice that difference. The stability label highlights entries that deserve an extra current check before a large deal.
