Choose the next rod by the bottleneck

If your current rod cannot hold the target fish, weight capacity is the first requirement. After that, compare luck and speed. Buying an expensive rod that still misses the required capacity does not solve the catch.

Recommended route logic

Start on Fisherman Island, then move through Kohana, Coral Reefs, Tropical Grove, Crater Island, Lost Isle, and Ancient Jungle as equipment improves. Ocean travel connects the route, but the exact best stop depends on your current collection target.
