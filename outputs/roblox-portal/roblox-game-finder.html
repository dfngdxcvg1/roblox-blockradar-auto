How BlockRadar chooses recommendations

The finder starts with the site's editorial safety score, then adds your age, group, time, mood, budget, and scare preferences. Live Roblox player activity is a small tie-breaker rather than the main answer. That keeps a popular but poor-fit game from automatically winning.
