Before you play

  1. Open the official Roblox page and confirm the experience name.
  2. Read the spending and scam notes before trading or buying passes.
  3. For younger players, preview chat behavior and scare level first.

Beginner route

  1. Learn one survivor role before trying the killer.
  2. Do objectives while another player takes chase.
  3. Treat pallets as limited team resources.
  4. Expect bugs because the official page labels the game alpha.

Parent notes

  1. The official maturity label is Mild, but the title and premise are explicitly violent.
  2. Player-controlled hunting can feel intense.
  3. This is better suited to teens than young children.
