Before you play

  1. Open the official Roblox page and confirm the experience name.
  2. Read the spending and scam notes before trading or buying passes.
  3. For younger players, preview chat behavior and scare level first.

Beginner route

  1. Read the official flashing-light warning before joining.
  2. Learn a support-oriented survivor first.
  3. Stay near teammates without stacking on one objective.
  4. Use headphones cautiously because loud audio is part of the warning.

Parent notes

  1. The official maturity label is Mild.
  2. The developer explicitly warns players with epilepsy about flashing lights.
  3. Horror, loud audio, and player-controlled killers make this a teen-focused choice.
