Beginner route

  1. Use daylight for resource runs and return before night pressure rises.
  2. Build beds early to improve day progression.
  3. Keep fire resources in reserve instead of spending everything.
  4. Assign gathering, building, and rescue roles in a group.

Parent notes

  1. The official maturity label is Minimal, but the presentation is intentionally tense.
  2. Darkness, pursuit, and implied threat can feel stronger than the label suggests.
  3. Known-friend co-op is safer than relying on public chat.
