How to read the result

Total value measures the reviewed estimate for every fruit on one side. Demand matters separately: a slightly lower total with consistently high-demand fruits may be easier to trade than a larger total of slow-moving items.

Trade safety checklist

  1. Keep every item inside Roblox's supported trade window.
  2. Reject trust trades, borrowed-item promises, fake middlemen, and off-platform payments.
  3. Recheck both sides before the final confirmation because items can be changed.
  4. Walk away from urgency. A real offer does not require your password or a website login.
